4-Step Quick Setup in Elgato Stream Deck App

1

Add "Website" Action

Open Elgato Stream Deck app. In the right action list, scroll to System and drag Website onto any vacant key.

2

Paste Webhook URL

Click the key in Stream Deck and paste the endpoint (e.g. http://localhost:3000/api/stream/next) into the URL field.

3

Check Background Option

Check the checkbox "Open in background" or "POST request" so your browser doesn't open a new tab.

4

Test Live in OBS

Press your physical key! The question slides on-air instantly with chime sound effects in OBS Studio.
